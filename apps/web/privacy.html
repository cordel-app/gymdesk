Two roles

As controller we handle the data of people who contact us or of Customer staff who hold an account: name, email, and the content of their messages. As processor we handle, on behalf of each gym (the Customer), the data of that gym's members: profile, bookings, memberships, payments, and training and nutrition data. The gym is the controller and decides its purposes. Use of the platform by a gym is covered by a data processing agreement.

Recipients

We use providers that process data for us: hosting and storage, authentication (Clerk), payment processing (Monei) and email. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

Data is kept while the contract is in force and afterwards for the time needed to meet legal obligations. A gym's member data is deleted or returned at the Customer's request when the contract ends.
