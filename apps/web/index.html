What Cordel Fitness App does

Members

Profiles, goals and progress, with staff roles and permissions per gym.

Classes and bookings

Calendar, capacity, waiting lists, reminders and trainer availability.

Memberships and payments

Membership plans, promotions, recurring billing and receipts, charged through a regulated payment provider.

Training and nutrition

Exercise library, training plans and nutrition plans assigned to each member.

Member app

A web and mobile app where members book, pay, follow their plan and track progress.

Multi-gym

One platform for several gyms and centers, each with its own branding.
